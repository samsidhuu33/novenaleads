How it works

Three steps, and you do none of them.

A buyer emails your agency

Inquiries from your website, portals or direct mail arrive in your usual inbox. Nothing changes for the buyer.

The assistant reads it

It pulls out the name, phone, request and urgency. If a detail is missing, the cell stays empty instead of guessing.

The lead lands in your sheet

One new row per inquiry, marked High, Medium or Low. Open the sheet and start with the top of the list.

Questions

Before you say yes.

Where does my inquiry data go?

Only into the Google Sheet you own. Before the pilot starts I explain which services read the emails and where they process data, so you can decide with your own data protection rules in mind.

Does it reply to my buyers?

No. It only reads and sorts. You keep talking to your buyers yourself. Automatic replies can be added later if you want them.

What if it gets a detail wrong?

The original email stays in your inbox, and the full message text is saved in the sheet next to the extracted fields, so you can always check.

What do I need to start?

A Gmail or Google Workspace inbox for inquiries and a Google account for the sheet. Using Outlook? Ask, and we will see if it can be done.
